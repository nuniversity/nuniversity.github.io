# Course Synthesis and Capstone

This final lesson integrates knowledge from all 12 previous lessons, challenges you to apply the concepts to real-world scenarios, and provides a roadmap for continued growth in the non-profit sector.

## Capstone Project: Choose One Option

### Option A: Organizational Assessment and Improvement Plan
**Context**: Assess your own organization (or one you know well) across key governance, financial, and program dimensions; develop a targeted improvement plan for the top 3 identified areas.

**Requirements**:
1. **Organization Overview** (1 page): Mission; type (OSCIP/Associação/Fundação); size; budget range; years of operation
2. **Governance Assessment** (1-2 pages):
   - Board composition and term limits
   - Governance model in use (Policy, Management, Hybrid, Cooperative)
   - Code of Ethics existence and implementation status
   - Conflict-of-interest policy and annual disclosure compliance
   - Board meeting frequency and effectiveness
3. **Financial Health Assessment** (1-2 pages):
   - Revenue diversification (percentage from each source: individual donations, corporate partnerships, government programs, foundation grants, earned income)
   - Expense ratio and program efficiency
   - Reserve ratio (months of operating expenses)
   - Key financial ratios analysis (current ratio, days cash on hand)
   - Recent audit findings and remediation status
4. **Program Effectiveness Assessment** (1-2 pages):
   - Logic Model or Theory of Change for one key program (or note if not formally developed)
   - Key outcome metrics and current performance
   - Stakeholder feedback summary (beneficiaries, staff, donors)
   - Improvement recommendations for the program
5. **Top 3 Improvement Priorities** (1 page each = total 3 pages):
   - Priority 1: Issue; impact; 90-day action plan; resources needed; success metrics
   - Priority 2: Issue; impact; 90-day action plan; resources needed; success metrics
   - Priority 3: Issue; impact; 90-day action plan; resources needed; success metrics
6. **90-Day Implementation Roadmap** (1 page):
   - Timeline with milestones; responsible parties; resource requirements; potential barriers; enablers; check-in schedule

### Option B: Strategic Plan Framework
**Context**: Develop a framework for a 3-year strategic plan for your organization or a hypothetical organization; include mission/vision/values refinement; strategic priorities; resource strategy; governance plan.

**Requirements**:
1. **Mission/Vision/Values Refinement** (1 page): Articulate clarity; alignment; any revisions; stakeholder input process
2. **3-Year Strategic Priorities** (1 page each = total 3 pages):
   - Priority 1: Problem/opportunity; goal; strategies; key actions; timeline; responsible party; metrics
   - Priority 2: Problem/opportunity; goal; strategies; key actions; timeline; responsible party; metrics
   - Priority 3: Problem/opportunity; goal; strategies; key actions; timeline; responsible party; metrics
3. **Resource Strategy** (1-2 pages): Revenue diversification 3-5 year plan; fundraising plan; financial sustainability targets; cost modeling; risk mitigation
4. **Governance Plan** (1-2 pages): Board composition roadmap; term limit implementation; recruitment plan; development plan; committee structure; evaluation process
5. **3-Year Implementation Timeline** (1 page): Gantt-style or milestone list; key months; decisions needed; resource milestones; evaluation checkpoints

### Option C: Theory of Change + SROI Analysis
**Context**: Develop a Theory of Change for one program; then conduct a streamlined SROI analysis; demonstrate the calculation process; present findings; recommend improvements.

**Requirements**:
1. **Theory of Change** (2-3 pages): Long-term goal; preconditions (3-5 levels with causal links); key assumptions; activities mapped to outcomes; stakeholder validation summary; diagram or graphic representation
2. **SROI Analysis** (3-4 pages): Investment summary; identified outcomes (4-6); evidence for each; monetization approach (conservative; ranges); SROI ratio calculation; sensitivity analysis (key assumptions tested); interpretation; limitations; stakeholder perspectives included
3. **Integration Commentary** (1 page): How the Theory of Change informed the SROI; what the SROI reveals; how findings will be used; credibility enhancements; next steps for impact improvement

This comprehensive course has taken you through the fundamental dimensions of non-profit organization management, from foundational definitions and sector comparisons to advanced operational, financial, and impact measurement practices. Key learning areas included:

- **Maintain 3-6 months operating reserves** as a liquidity buffer against uncertainty
- **Implement clear governance** with formal conflict-of-interest policies; term limits; board development; regular self-assessment
- **Use Logic Models** for program planning; Theory of Change for strategic; SROI for investment cases; mix methods for credibility
- **Navigate Brazil's Third Sector Law** strategically: choose the right organizational type (OSCIP for federal resources; Associação for simplicity; Fundação for endowments)
- **Leverage tax incentives** strategically: Rouanet for cultural projects; Pixote for science/technology; understand individual (6% IRPF) and corporate (2% taxable income) deduction limits
- **Adopt donor-centric fundraising** with truthfulness, respect, equity, privacy, and financial transparency as core ethical pillars
- **Measure what matters** using mixed methods; triangulate quantitative scale with qualitative depth; be transparent about limitations; use findings for continuous improvement
