## Course Description

A deep, comprehensive exploration of the mind and behavior. This course presents psychology as the **science of mental processes and behavior** - connecting brain function to human experience through interactive simulations and engaging case studies.

### Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, students will be able to:
1. Understand research methods in psychology
2. Explore biological bases of behavior
3. Understand sensation and perception
4. Explore learning and memory
5. Understand cognition, emotion, and motivation
6. Explore personality, development, and psychopathology

---

## Real-World Examples

### Biological Psychology

**Example 1: Brain Plasticity**
The brain changes with experience. Understanding neuroplasticity helps us treat brain injuries and improve learning.

**Example 2: Neurotransmitters**
Chemicals in the brain affect mood, motivation, and behavior. Understanding neurotransmitters helps us develop better treatments for mental illness.

### Learning and Memory

**Example 3: Classical Conditioning**
Pavlov's dogs learned to salivate at the sound of a bell. Understanding conditioning helps us treat phobias and addictions.

**Example 4: Memory Techniques**
Memory champions use techniques like memory palaces. Understanding memory helps us learn more effectively.

### Cognition

**Example 5: Cognitive Biases**
Our brains take shortcuts that can lead to errors. Understanding biases helps us make better decisions.

**Example 6: Decision Making**
From buying groceries to choosing careers, we make decisions constantly. Understanding decision-making helps us choose wisely.

### Emotion and Motivation

**Example 7: Emotional Intelligence**
Understanding emotions helps us navigate social situations and build relationships.

**Example 8: Motivation**
Understanding what drives us helps us achieve goals and improve well-being.

### Personality and Development

**Example 9: Personality Traits**
Personality affects how we think, feel, and behave. Understanding personality helps us understand ourselves and others.

**Example 10: Lifelong Development**
We change throughout our lives. Understanding development helps us support healthy growth.
